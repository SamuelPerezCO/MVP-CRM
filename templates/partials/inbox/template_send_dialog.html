{% comment %} The Enviar plantilla modal for one conversation -- what core.views.inbox_template_send answers a GET with. The "Enviar plantilla" button in the composer fetches it into #tpl-send-slot, and shell.js opens any swapped-in [data-open-on-swap] dialog, so it is always fresh: the list of plantillas is read at the moment of opening, never cached in the thread. The form posts to the same URL and aims at the thread, exactly like the composer does: a sent plantilla is just another outbound message there. data-close-on-success is the existing shell.js rule that closes the dialog once that swap succeeds. A rejected submit (a variable left blank) comes back 422 with HX-Retarget at #tpl-send-body, so the form -- and what was typed -- survives. Context: active_conversation, entries, selected_id, send_form_error {% endcomment %}
