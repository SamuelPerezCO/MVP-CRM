The button carries two independent hooks that fire on the same click: - data-dialog-open="plantilla-send-dialog": shell.js keeps one click listener on the document that looks for this attribute, finds the with that id (send_dialog.html, included once by panels/clientes.html) and calls showModal() on it. The dialog opens at once, showing whatever its body holds -- the "Cargando…" placeholder on first open, the previous row's form afterwards -- until the response below lands. - hx-get: htmx (a button's default trigger is click) issues a GET in the background to the URL named plantilla_send_form in core/urls.py; the url tag reverses that name plus this row's client.pk into "/plantillas/enviar//". core.views.plantilla_send_form answers with send_form.html rendered for this client. hx-target="#plantilla-send-body" is the element the answer lands in (an id selector, looked up in the whole document) and hx-swap="innerHTML" means it replaces that element's contents, not the element itself. Rows that arrive through a paging swap behave the same: htmx processes hx-* attributes on any content it swaps in, and the shell.js listener sits on the document, so nothing is re-bound. can_receive_template is a property on core.models.Client: a phone number and a channel of "" or "whatsapp".
